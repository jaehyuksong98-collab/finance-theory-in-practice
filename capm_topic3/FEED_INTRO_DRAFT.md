Does CAPM's “higher beta, higher expected return” show up in real data?

I revisited one of my Investment Analysis lectures using 63 years of U.S. beta-sorted portfolio returns. The result helped me separate two ideas that are easy to mix up: beta describes market exposure, while future returns remain uncertain.

The Article walks through the capital market line, security market line, beta, alpha, the data test and its limits. I also wrote reproducible Python code so the test can be updated as new data arrives.
