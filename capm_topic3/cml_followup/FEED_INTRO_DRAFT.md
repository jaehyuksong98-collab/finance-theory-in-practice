The efficient-frontier diagram in class led me to a practical question: does a portfolio selected from past data remain a good choice in the following year?

I tested a rolling 10-year selection against a simple equal mix using 53 subsequent July–June years of U.S. beta-sorted portfolio data. The result depends on the estimation window. The Article shows the decision rule, the observed risk and return, and the limits of this historical test. Reproducible code and a short methods report accompany it.
